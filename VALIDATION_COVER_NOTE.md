# Would you read this? (A quick ask)



**The ask:** read these sample chapters (each is a 5–10 minute read) and tell us honestly.

1. **The Founding** (1763–1789)
2. **Slavery & Emancipation** (1619–1865)
3. **Reconstruction** (1865–1877)
4. **Standard Oil** (1870–1911) — corporate power
5. **Civil Rights** (1896–1965)
6. **The Cold War** (1947–1991)

**Three questions — please be blunt:**
1. **Did it read as fair, or did it feel like it was pushing a side?** (This is the one that matters most. If you lean left, did it feel too soft on America? If you lean right, too hard? We want to hear either.)
2. **Did anything strike you as factually wrong or missing?**
3. **Would you use this — for your kids, your classroom, or yourself? Would you pay for it?**

No wrong answers. Harsh feedback is the most valuable kind. Thank you.
