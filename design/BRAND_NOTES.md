# Brand notes — the wax seal

_2026-07-26._

## The seal is a mark of record, not of authority

Not "approved by authority" — **entered, preserved, and traceable**. It confirms
the record and its provenance; it does not declare the meaning. That distinction
is the whole product, compressed into one object.

## Role: secondary asset, not the everyday logo

| asset | use |
|---|---|
| **Wordmark / HL monogram** | the everyday logo |
| **Wax seal** | citations, completed ledger entries, verified source bundles, loading moments, splash, premium print |
| **Stamp version** | one-colour, flatter, legible at small sizes |
| **Hero version** | rich oxblood wax, brass or blind-embossed HL |

The full-page layout (`historys-ledger-fullpage.png`) is closer to the real app
than the brand board — restrained, editorial, literate. The seal can live in that
world without tipping it into costume drama.

## Wording — decided: **"Entered into the Ledger"**

Rejected: **"Verified truth"** — that is a judgment, and judgment is the one
thing this product hands back to the reader.

Rejected: **"Source Record Complete"** — it collides with the app's own
**WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN** panel. A screen that counts open questions and a seal
that says *complete* cannot both be honest. "Entered" claims only what actually
happened.

**Rule that follows: never use "complete" as a status anywhere in this product.**
Records are provisional by design; the UI already says so.

## ⚠️ Two corrections outstanding on the brand board

1. **The record screen's seal badge currently reads "VERIFIED · TRUTH ON RECORD"**
   (next to `RECORD ID: HL-1848-07-19-001`). That is the exact wording this note
   rejects. The advice is right; the mockup hasn't caught up.
2. **The logo marks say "EST. 2024."** The name was locked 2026-07-13. On a brand
   whose promise is "Truth on Record," a wrong founding date on the seal is the
   detail a critic would most enjoy finding.

# App homepage mockup — 2026-07-26

`design/app-homepage-mockup-2026-07-26.png`. This is the strongest artefact the
project has: the four pillars become the navigation —

**Established Facts · Primary Sources · Conflicting Accounts · What Remains Unknown**

— which turns the thesis into the product's spine rather than a claim about it.
"Ask Atticus: cites sources, or says when the record is incomplete" states the
honesty rule on the front door. Headline **"Read the record. Decide for
yourself."** is better than "The record speaks. You decide." — it addresses the
reader directly and asks something of them.

## ⚠️ Factual errors in the sample entries — fix before reuse

Two of the three "From the Ledger" cards carry wrong dates:

| card | shown | correct |
|---|---|---|
| The Surrender at Appomattox | April 17, 1865 | **April 9, 1865** |
| The Emancipation Proclamation | May 20, 1865 | **January 1, 1863** (preliminary Sept 22, 1862) |

Juneteenth (June 19, 1865) is correct. The Douglass quote is genuine and
correctly attributed to the *Narrative*, 1845.

They are placeholders, not shipped content — but on a product whose entire claim
is provenance, a wrong date in the shop window is the error that ends you.

## ⚠️ "EST. 2024" / "© 2024" — still wrong, now in two places

The wax seal reads EST. 2024 and the footer reads © 2024. The name was locked
**2026-07-13**. A truth-on-record brand should not carry a fabricated founding
date.

## ⚠️ It is an APP homepage, not a landing page

Search, Browse the Archive, View All Entries, Ask Atticus, and seven topic
categories — none of it exists. Shipped as-is at historysledger.com, every click
is a dead end and the site is making claims it cannot honour. Keep it as the
**build target**; the page that goes up now has to be honest about being early.

Also note the topic row promises seven areas (incl. Industrial America, World
Wars, Modern America) where six chapters exist and don't map onto them cleanly.
