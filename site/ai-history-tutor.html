What is Atticus?

Atticus is the guide on History’s Ledger. He answers from the documents this site holds. If a page is not held, he stops. That refusal is the product working. You can try him on any open entry. A free Reconstruction week is a clear place to start: Reconstruction — The Promise and the Betrayal.

Does Atticus cite its sources?

Yes, when the document is held. He pulls the held source or image. When the record stops, Atticus stops. That refusal is the product working.

How is cite-or-stop different from a general chatbot?

A general chatbot will keep talking. Atticus only answers from the held corpus, or he goes quiet. Families can open the same cards he cites.
