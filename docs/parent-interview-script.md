# Parent interview script — Family year pricing

_Use with 10–20 real parents (homeschool, classical/CM, APUSH-curious). 15–20 minutes. Not a survey form — a conversation._

## Goal

Learn **willingness to pay** at three prices and what would make Family year feel worth it — without selling hard.

## Setup (2 min)

- Send link: https://historysledger.com/  
- Ask them to open one US entry + try Atticus once (or share screen).
- Optional: https://historysledger.com/read/modern-wars/ for WWII.

**Script open:**

> Thanks for looking. History’s Ledger is primary-source history with an AI guide that is built not to invent. Free shelves stay free. We’re designing a **Family year** subscription — not charging yet. I want your honest reaction, including “I wouldn’t pay.”

## Show, then ask (5 min)

Walk through (or let them drive):

1. Achievement / cost side by side on one entry.  
2. Tap a verified source / Atticus “open the Declaration.”  
3. Ask Atticus something off-shelf (e.g. Sparta) — hear the clean refusal.

**Check:**

> What did that feel like compared to a normal textbook or ChatGPT?

## Value bullets (2 min)

Read the three paid promises:

1. Full open shelves for the household  
2. Atticus that pulls held sources (and images when rights allow)  
3. School-year path + parent guide (when ready)

**Check:**

> Which of those three matters most for your family? Which least?

## Price ladder (5 min) — do not negotiate mid-ladder

Ask in this order; wait for a full answer each time:

1. **At $49 / family / year** — would you seriously consider it? Why / why not?  
2. **At $79 / year** — same.  
3. **At $119 / year** — same.

Then:

> What would have to be true for $119 to feel fair?  
> What would make even $49 feel like a no?

Optional:

> Would you rather pay once per school year, or monthly?

## Curriculum fit (3 min)

> Are you comparing this to a curriculum you already buy? Which ones?  
> Do you need AP / standards language, or “living documents” language?  
> How many kids, what ages?

## Close

> If Family year opened in a few months with early price locked for waitlist people, would you put your email down?  
> (Point to https://historysledger.com/family.html — optional live join.)

Thank them. **No pitch deck.** Note quotes verbatim after the call.

## Scorecard (fill after each call)

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Profile | ages / classical / APUSH / co-op |
| Max serious price | $49 / $79 / $119 / none |
| Must-have for paid | shelves / Atticus / year path / guides / other |
| Killer objection | |
| Memorable quote | |
| Waitlist? | yes / no |

## After 10 calls

- If most die at $49 → product not clear enough yet; do not open pay.  
- If $79 is comfortable and $119 needs “full year path” → **Family ~$79**, raise when year path ships.  
- If $119 is fine for classical + APUSH parents → you can test **$99–119** with stronger packaging.

Record raw counts; don’t average away the split between “homeschool curriculum buyers” and “curious adults.”
